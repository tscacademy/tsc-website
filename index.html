Frequently asked questions

Everything you'd want to know before starting

What happens during the TSC Athlete Assessment?

A video session where we look at your athlete's physical performance, mental performance and overall development, then walk through what we're seeing directly with you — followed by a written roadmap.

Is this a diagnosis, therapy, or medical service?

No. TSC provides performance coaching and mentorship — not a diagnosis, treatment plan, or clinical service. If your child's situation calls for that, we'll tell you directly and point you toward the right professional.

When do we receive the written roadmap?

Within 72 hours of your session. If it isn't in your inbox by then, we refund the full $125.

Do we have to continue with TSC after the assessment?

No. There's no obligation. The assessment stands on its own, and you keep the roadmap either way.

How does the founding rate work?

The $125 Assessment is open to any family, any time. The 3-month program pricing shown ($750 / $1,350) is our founding rate for the first 10 families who join a program — after that, standard 3-month pricing applies ($950 / $1,750). Enroll in either program within 7 days of your evaluation and the full $125 is credited toward it.

Are sessions online, and what ages do you work with?

Yes, every session runs over video call. We work with young athletes roughly 8 to 18 years old.

Which sports do you work with?

We focus on tennis and football (soccer). Everything runs online, so families can join from anywhere. Prices are in USD.
