FAQ

Quick answers

What happens in the Assessment?

A video session where we look at your athlete's physical and mental performance and talk it through with you. Within 72 hours you get a written roadmap with the priorities.

Do we have to continue after it?

No. You keep the roadmap either way. If you join a program within 7 days, the full $125 is credited.

How does the founding price work?

The first 10 families to join a program pay $750 or $1,350 for 3 months instead of $950 or $1,750. You can pay in full or in 3 monthly payments.

Is this therapy or medical care?

No. It's performance coaching. If something needs a doctor, physio or psychologist, we tell you directly.

How do sessions work?

All online by video, scheduled around school and training, so you can join from anywhere.

Which ages and sports?

Roughly 8 to 18, mainly tennis and football (soccer). Other sports: ask us.
