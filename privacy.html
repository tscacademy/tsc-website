Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

TSC Academy is part of The Sport Class. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or book a TSC Athlete Assessment, why we collect it, and your rights. We work with young athletes, so we take particular care with information about children.

Who we are

TSC Academy, part of The Sport Class, is responsible for the personal information described here (the data controller). For anything about your data, email info@tscacademyofficial.com.

What we collect

  • Booking details. When you book an Assessment through our booking calendar (Google Calendar), we receive the name and email you enter and any notes you add.
  • Information about your athlete. During the Assessment and any program, a parent or guardian may share information such as the athlete's age, sport, training, goals, and physical and mental-performance observations. We only collect this from, or with the agreement of, a parent or guardian.
  • Messages. Anything you send us by email or social media.
  • Website usage. If you accept cookies, Meta (Facebook) Pixel records visits to this page and clicks on our booking buttons. If you decline, it doesn't.

Why we use it

  • To run the Assessment, write your athlete's roadmap and deliver any program you choose (to provide the service you asked for).
  • To reply to you and send practical information about your sessions.
  • To understand which of our posts and ads bring families to this website, only if you accepted cookies (your consent).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use information about your athlete for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run TSC Academy: Google (booking calendar, email and video calls), Meta (website measurement, only with your consent) and Netlify (website hosting). Some of these providers store data outside the UK, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep Assessment and program records for as long as we work with your family and for up to two years after our last session, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you or your athlete, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@tscacademyofficial.com. If you're in the UK and unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website only sets measurement cookies from Meta if you click "Accept cookies". To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and the cookie banner will appear again.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
